Production workflow

How we work.

The scope, review rounds and outputs are agreed for each brief.

  1. Discovery

    Share the audience, goal, format, budget and timeline. Together we define the scope and the decisions that will guide production.

  2. Concept

    We explore references and propose a visual direction. You review the concept before detailed work begins.

  3. Design & create

    We develop the agreed direction through sketches, layouts, models or motion tests suited to the brief.

  4. Review & refine

    Review the work against the brief and gather feedback. We refine the details within the agreed scope.

  5. Deliver

    We prepare the agreed files, check the formats and hand over the assets for their intended channels.
