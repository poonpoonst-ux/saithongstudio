Privacy policy.

How this website handles your project inquiry and language preference.

Information you share

The project form collects your name, email, country, service, budget range, timeline, project description and consent. You can also share a company name, phone number and reference link. Please send only information relevant to the project.

How the studio uses it

SAITHONG STUDIO uses inquiry details to understand the brief, discuss scope and timing, prepare a quotation and follow up on your request.

Form processing and email

On the live Netlify site, Netlify Forms processes and stores submissions. When email notifications are enabled, inquiry details are also delivered to the studio’s designated inbox. Hosting and email providers process the information needed to deliver these services.

Language preference

When you change the language, the site saves your choice in your browser’s local storage under saithong-language. You can remove this preference by clearing this site’s browser data.

Questions about your data

Use the project inquiry form to ask about access, correction or deletion of information you submitted. Include the email address used for your inquiry so the studio can locate the record and verify your request.
